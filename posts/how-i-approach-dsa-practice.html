How I Approach DSA Practice

May 24, 2026 • 4 min read

1. Pattern Blocks Over Random Solving

I split sessions by pattern: sliding window, binary search, BFS/DFS, DP, and so on. This creates repetition and helps me recognize structures faster during contests.

2. Track Mistakes Explicitly

For each difficult question, I write one short note: what I missed, what optimization was needed, and what signal I should spot earlier next time.

3. Time-Bounded Revision

I reserve weekly revision blocks to revisit solved problems and confirm whether I can still derive the approach without hints.
